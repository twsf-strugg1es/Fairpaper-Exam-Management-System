# Fairpaper: 30 days on LinkedIn and X

**Goal:** waitlist sign-ups from coaching centers, schools and hiring teams. Every post ends with the waitlist link (`[link]` below).

**Rules:**
- Every claim is something Fairpaper does today. Never write that cheating is impossible: say it is **harder**, and **flagged for a person to review**.
- No invented numbers. Post real figures only when you have them (waitlist size, a pilot's results).
- Bangladesh first, global wording. Post in English; Bengali versions for Tuesdays and Fridays are marked BN.
- One image or clip per post. "Screen" means a screenshot from the app.

**Cadence:** LinkedIn once a day at 9:00 Dhaka time (teachers and owners). X once or twice a day, the short version.

## A prompt for writing more posts

Paste this into any AI assistant when you need fresh posts:

> You write social posts for Fairpaper, an online exam platform by Kori for coaching centers, schools and hiring teams (launching in Bangladesh, built for anywhere). Rules: never claim cheating is impossible (say "harder" and "flagged for review"), never invent numbers, plain words, no hype, no emojis except one at most. Write [a LinkedIn post of about 80 words / an X post under 260 characters] on: [topic]. End with: Join the waitlist: [link]

## Five short clips (9:16 for Reels and Shorts, 16:9 for LinkedIn)

Record from the real app (staging), 15–30 seconds each, with captions burned in (most people watch muted) and no voice needed. The plan is to build them with HyperFrames from these storyboards.

1. **Leave the tab (15 s).**
   - 0–3 s: a student answering; caption "A student is writing an exam".
   - 3–7 s: they switch tabs and the exam blurs; caption "They leave the tab".
   - 7–11 s: the examiner's report shows the exit with its time; caption "It's recorded".
   - 11–15 s: logo and waitlist link; caption "Flagged, never accused".
2. **Grade a pile in minutes (20 s).**
   - The grade-by-question screen.
   - Click half marks, press Enter, the next paper appears, repeat three times.
   - Caption: "One question across every paper".
3. **One question at a time (15 s).**
   - A per-question countdown from 0:10 to 0:00; the next question opens.
   - Caption: "No going back".
4. **Set up in 5 minutes (30 s, sped up).**
   - Excel import preview, then New exam, then Publish, then the student dashboard showing the exam.
5. **A fair exam in 30 seconds (30 s).**
   - Shuffled options side by side for two students, the offline badge, an answer saved, the blur, submit, the result card and the merit list.
